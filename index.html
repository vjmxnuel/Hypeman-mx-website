MEET HYPEMAN MX.

Hypeman Mx is a club hypeman, MC, event host and entertainment personality focused on creating memorable experiences through music, crowd engagement and stage presence.

From building his entertainment journey in Ibadan to expanding his brand in Lagos, Mx is driven by a passion for nightlife, entertainment and connecting with audiences.

His vision is to take the Mx experience to clubs, festivals, concerts, private events and international stages.
